# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PhotoCue is a tool for documentary photographers and visual storytellers. We are
committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community and to the **dignity and
safety of the people and communities that appear in photographs**.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, curious, and specific.
- Assume good faith and give constructive feedback.
- Centre the perspective and consent of photographed subjects.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Content that exploits, endangers, or stereotypes any community.
- Prompt contributions that assume identity, wealth, danger, or politics, or that
  encourage intrusive or non-consensual photography.
- Sharing others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in
public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn or ban participants,
for behaviour that violates this code. To report an issue, contact the
maintainers privately via the repository's contact channels.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
