# Privacy policy

PhotoCue is built to keep your work yours.

## What we do not do

- No account, sign-up, or email address is required.
- No advertising, no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics by default.
- We do not sell or share your data — there is no server to send it to.

## What is stored, and where

Everything is stored **locally on your device**:

- **Stories, saved prompts, field notes, and optional thumbnails** — in your
  browser's IndexedDB.
- **Preferences and your last entered location** — in `localStorage`.
- **Prompt history** (used to keep prompts fresh) — in IndexedDB.
- **An anonymous installation seed** — a random value that varies your prompt
  sequence. It never leaves your device and is excluded from any backup you
  choose to *share*.

Optional photo thumbnails are stored locally and are only included if you
explicitly export or share a story.

## Location

- PhotoCue works fully without any location.
- If you use **Use My Location**, we ask for permission only at that moment and
  only after showing what will happen.
- We request an **approximate** location and use it only to derive a neutral
  label. **Exact coordinates are never stored or transmitted.**
- You can clear your saved location, or turn off remembering it, in **Settings**.

## Analytics

If analytics are ever enabled, they are **off by default**, opt-in, aggregated,
and contain no personal data or third parties.

## Your controls (Settings → Your data)

- **Export backup** — download a versioned JSON file (shareable version strips
  the installation seed).
- **Import backup** — review a summary before anything is imported.
- **Clear saved location** — remove location data only.
- **Delete all data** — permanently erase everything PhotoCue stored on the
  device.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
